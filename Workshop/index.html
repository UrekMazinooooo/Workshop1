Study Tips

How to Prepare for Exams

A little study every day works better than studying everything the night before.

  • Make a daily timetable and follow it
  • Study for 25 minutes, then take a 5 minute break
  • Write short notes and revise them often
  • Sleep well before the exam

Homework Routine

  1. Write down your homework in a diary
  2. Start with the hardest subject
  3. Finish each task before playing
  4. Pack your bag for tomorrow
